# Nourish Design System — Clinical Warmth

## Colors
- Background: #FAFAF8
- Card: #FFFFFF
- Text Primary: #1C1C1E
- Text Secondary: #6B6B6B
- Accent: #C1604F (terracotta)
- Border: #EBEBEA
- Subtle: #F5F5F3

## Typography
- Headings: "DM Serif Display" — elegant, warm serif
- Body/UI: "DM Sans" — clean, legible sans
- Numbers/Metrics: "DM Mono" — precise, clinical feel

## Spacing
- Base: 16px
- Cards: 16px padding
- Section gap: 24px

## Components
- Cards: white bg, 2px solid #EBEBEA, border-radius 16px, NO shadows
- Buttons: accent fill (#C1604F), white text, border-radius 12px, min-height 48px
- Inputs: min-height 48px, 2px border #EBEBEA, focus border #C1604F
- Tab Bar: 60px height, white, 1px top border #EBEBEA

## Principles
- NO gradients
- NO drop shadows
- NO Inter/Roboto
- Mobile-first (max-width 430px centered)
- Warm, clinical, trustworthy
